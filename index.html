3. Information We Collect

a) Locally Stored Game Data

All game data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to external servers:

  • Game statistics (scores, completion times, win/loss records, streaks)
  • Game preferences (sound, haptic feedback, visual settings)
  • Puzzle progress and saved games
  • Local user identifier (randomly generated UUID, not linked to your personal identity)
  • Achievement progress and difficulty ratings

All local data is encrypted with AES-256 encryption (SQLCipher) and is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

b) Data Collected by Third-Party Advertising Services

Our app displays advertisements through Google AdMob. The AdMob SDK may automatically collect the following types of data, including Personal Information and Device or Other IDs:

  • Advertising ID (Ad ID) — a unique, user-resettable device identifier used for personalized advertising (classified as "Device or Other IDs")
  • Device identifiers — device-specific identifiers used for analytics and ad serving
  • IP address (which may be used to infer approximate location)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks, impressions)
  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • App usage data related to ad serving

The Advertising ID is collected via the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission. This data is collected and processed by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy.

c) App Permissions and Their Purposes

SudokuXYZ requests the following device permissions:

  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET) — Required for loading and displaying advertisements
  • Network State (android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — Used to check network connectivity before loading ads
  • Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE) — Used for haptic feedback during gameplay
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — Used by Google AdMob to serve advertisements and collect the Ad ID

The following permissions are automatically included by the Google AdMob SDK:

  • Ad Services Ad ID (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID) — Android Privacy Sandbox: access to advertising identifier
  • Ad Services Attribution (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION) — Android Privacy Sandbox: ad attribution measurement
  • Ad Services Topics (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) — Android Privacy Sandbox: interest-based advertising topics
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK) — Keeps screen active during video ad playback
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE) — Required for background ad processing

7. Data Storage and Security

  • All game data is stored locally on your device only
  • The database is encrypted with AES-256 via SQLCipher with a 16 KB page size
  • Data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer
  • No game data is stored on or transmitted to external servers
  • We do not operate any backend servers or cloud services

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy:

  • Local game data: Retained on your device until you choose to delete it (via Profile → “Delete All Data”), reset your data (via Settings), or uninstall the app. We do not retain any copy of this data on external servers
  • Advertising data: Collected and retained by Google according to Google’s data retention policies. Google may retain advertising-related data for varying periods depending on the type of data and your account settings

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data at any time. See Section 11 (“How to Delete Your Data”) for detailed instructions.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have specific rights regarding your personal data under applicable privacy laws. Below we outline your rights under various regulations. You have the right to know whether your personal data is being collected, used, shared, or sold.

For EU/EEA Residents (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access (Article 15): You have the right to request access to the personal data we collect about you
  • Right to rectification (Article 16): You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
  • Right to erasure (Article 17): You have the right to request deletion of your personal data (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Right to restriction (Article 18): You have the right to request restriction of processing of your personal data
  • Right to data portability (Article 20): You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Right to object (Article 21): You have the right to object to processing of your personal data
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority

For California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), you have the following rights:

  • Right to know: You have the right to know what personal information is collected about you and whether your personal information is sold or shared with third parties
  • Right to delete: You have the right to request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to correct: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Right to opt-out: You have the right to opt-out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. We do not sell your personal data
  • Right to non-discrimination: You have the right to not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights

For Brazilian Residents (LGPD)

Under the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to confirmation: You have the right to confirmation of the existence of processing of your personal data
  • Right to access: You have the right to request access to your personal data
  • Right to correction: You have the right to request correction of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated personal data
  • Right to deletion: You have the right to request anonymization, blocking, or deletion of unnecessary or excessive personal data
  • Right to portability: You have the right to request portability of your personal data
  • Right to information: You have the right to know which entities your personal data is shared with

For Virginia Residents (VCDPA)

Under the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA), you have the following rights:

  • Right to access: You have the right to request access to your personal data
  • Right to correction: You have the right to request correction of inaccuracies in your personal data
  • Right to deletion: You have the right to request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to portability: You have the right to obtain a copy of your personal data in a portable, readily usable format
  • Right to opt-out: You have the right to opt out of (i) targeted advertising, (ii) the sale of your personal data, and (iii) profiling in furtherance of decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects

For detailed instructions on how to exercise your opt-out rights, see Section 10 (“How to Exercise Your Opt-Out Rights”) below.

10. How to Exercise Your Opt-Out Rights

Under the VCDPA, CCPA, and other applicable privacy laws, you have the right to opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, and profiling. Here is how you can exercise each of these rights:

(i) Opt Out of Targeted Advertising

You can opt out of personalized (targeted) advertisements through any of the following methods:

  • In-App: Go to Settings → Ad Preferences within SudokuXYZ to manage your advertising consent preferences
  • Google Ad Settings: Visit https://adssettings.google.com to manage your Google ad personalization preferences
  • Device Settings: Adjust your device’s advertising settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device). You may also reset or delete your Advertising ID from your device settings
  • Consent Dialog: When you open SudokuXYZ, a consent dialog may appear allowing you to manage your ad preferences directly

(ii) Opt Out of the Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. The only data sharing occurs through the Google AdMob SDK for advertising purposes, as described in Section 6 (“Third-Party Services”). If you consider this sharing to constitute a “sale” under applicable law, you can opt out using the methods described in section (i) above.

(iii) Opt Out of Profiling

We do not engage in profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on users. Our app does not make automated decisions about users based on personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, or for any questions about opting out, you may also contact us at btmaapps@gmail.com.

11. How to Delete Your Data

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data. You can delete your data in the following ways:

  1. In-App: Go to Profile → “Delete All Data” to permanently erase all game data, settings, and preferences from your device

Since we do not store any data on external servers, deleting local data or uninstalling the app completely removes all your information.
