Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2025-12-29

This privacy policy applies to the SudokuXYZ mobile application (the “App”).

1. Information Collection and Use

The App does not require user registration and does not collect, store, or share personally identifiable information (such as your name or email address) through its own code.

2. Advertising and Analytics (Third-Party SDKs)

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you and analyze app performance. These services include:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying ads. It may collect Advertising IDs, approximate location, and usage data.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics: For monitoring app stability and performance. It collects crash logs, diagnostics, and app performance data to help us improve the user experience.

All data collected by these SDKs is encrypted in transit (using HTTPS).

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. However, the third-party providers mentioned above (Google) receive certain device-level data as part of their service operation.

4. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Since the App does not collect personal identity information or have user accounts, we do not store personal data to be deleted. You can restrict the collection of advertising identifiers through your Android device settings.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
