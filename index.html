Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2025-12-23

This privacy policy applies to the Sudoku mobile application (the “App”).

Information Collection and Use

The App does not require user registration and does not collect, store, or share personally identifiable information.

The App does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or device files.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner ads and rewarded video ads using Google AdMob. While the App itself does not collect personal data, AdMob may collect and process device information such as advertising identifiers, approximate location (derived from IP), and usage data to provide and improve ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud.

Google’s policies: Google Privacy Policy

AdMob info: How AdMob uses data

Data Sharing

The App does not share personal data with third parties because the App does not collect personal data. However, third-party advertising services (AdMob) may process device data as described above.

Data Retention

The App does not store user accounts or personal data on its own servers. Any ad-related data is handled by AdMob under Google’s policies.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Your Choices

You can reset your device’s advertising identifier and control ad personalization in your device settings (where available).
