Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 3, 2026
Last Updated: February 3, 2026

SudokuXYZ ("we", "us", "our") is a puzzle game developed by btmaapps for Android devices. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

1. Data Controller

btmaapps
Email: btmaapps@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

a) Locally Stored Game Data

All game data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to external servers:

  • Game statistics (scores, completion times, win/loss records, streaks)
  • Game preferences (sound, haptic feedback, visual settings)
  • Puzzle progress and saved games
  • Local user identifier (randomly generated UUID, not linked to your personal identity)
  • Achievement progress and difficulty ratings

All local data is encrypted with AES-256 encryption (SQLCipher) and is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

b) Data Collected by Third-Party Advertising Services

Our app displays advertisements through Google AdMob. The AdMob SDK may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers (including Advertising ID)
  • IP address (which may be used to infer approximate location)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks, impressions)
  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • App usage data related to ad serving

This data is collected and processed by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Legitimate interest: Displaying advertisements to support the free availability of the app
  • Consent: For personalized advertising, where applicable under your local laws
  • Contract performance: Providing the game service, saving your progress, and maintaining your preferences

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Local game data: To save your game progress, maintain your preferences, and display your statistics
  • Advertising data: To display relevant advertisements and support the free availability of the app

5. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Google LLC)

  • Purpose: Displaying advertisements
  • Data processed: Device identifiers, IP address, ad interaction data
  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Ad personalization opt-out: https://adssettings.google.com

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • All game data is stored locally on your device only
  • The database is encrypted with AES-256 via SQLCipher with a 16 KB page size
  • Data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer
  • No game data is stored on or transmitted to external servers
  • We do not operate any backend servers or cloud services

7. Data Retention

  • Local game data: Retained on your device until you choose to delete it or uninstall the app
  • Advertising data: Subject to Google's data retention policies

8. Your Rights

For EU/EEA Residents (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability
  • Object to processing
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority

For California Residents (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt-out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell your data)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

For Brazilian Residents (LGPD)

Under the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, you have the right to:

  • Confirmation of data processing
  • Access to your data
  • Correction of incomplete or inaccurate data
  • Anonymization, blocking, or deletion of unnecessary data
  • Data portability
  • Information about entities with which your data is shared

For Virginia Residents (VCDPA)

Under the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccuracies in your data
  • Delete your personal data
  • Obtain a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Opt out of targeted advertising

9. How to Delete Your Data

You can delete your data in the following ways:

  1. In-App: Go to Profile → "Delete All Data" to permanently erase all game data, settings, and preferences from your device
  2. Uninstall: Removing the app automatically deletes all locally stored data
  3. Contact Us: Email btmaapps@gmail.com for assistance

Since we do not store any data on external servers, deleting local data or uninstalling the app completely removes all your information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for general audiences and does not target children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (as defined by COPPA) or under 16 (as defined by GDPR). The only data collection is through the AdMob advertising SDK, which is managed by Google. If you believe a child has provided personal data through our app, please contact us at btmaapps@gmail.com.
